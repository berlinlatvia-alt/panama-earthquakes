Plain-language glossary

What are hectopascals (hPa)?
Air weight pressing on water. Normal pressure is — 1013 hPa. In a storm or cyclone pressure drops below 1005 hPa. Water «bulges» up 1 cm per 1 hPa drop. Today 1012 hPa — no storm, no surge.
Chart datum vs mean sea level:
The 4.22 m value is measured from chart datum. Relative to mean sea level it is only +1.77 meters! To the berm crest (+4.2 m) there remained more than 2.2 m (7 ft!) of safe height.

Checklist: read the tide without a phone

1. Beach morning-glory on the rocks:
Dense clover-shaped leaves on the breakwater. This plant dies in salt water and growsstrictly above the normal tide. If water covers its roots — the tide is abnormal. In your photo the grass is dry and 1.5 m above water.
2. Sand spit mid-channel:
At a normal tide the spit is visible and more than 1 m high. At a dangerous kingtide it disappears completely.
3. Wave overtopping the rocks:
Normal spray from 0.5 m waves at tidal peak — is normal. A dangerous wave (Mar de Fondo) — means heavy 2–3 m swells every 16–20 seconds sweeping breakwater stones away.

November (1 month): Peak rain, thunderstorm squalls

In January the north wind blows offshore at 30–45 km/h. The ocean at the beachbecomes mirror-smooth and rain and mold disappear completely.
